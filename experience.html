What is real, and what is illustrated?

The prompted sentences, partial predictions and final predictions come from the published dataset. The character, dialogue, room, implant placement and signal animation are educational illustrations.

Audio is browser speech synthesis, not T12’s recorded or reconstructed voice. The replay does not run a decoder live or measure Neuralink’s performance.

Research details and recording sites

Source: Willett et al. (2023), A high-performance speech neuroprosthesis; Dryad sentences dataset, selected file t12.2022.06.14_sentences.mat. The selected go periods contain 188 and 117 time bins of 20 ms, respectively.

The study recorded arrays in areas 6v and 44; speech-decoding analyses used 6v recordings. These research locations do not specify Neuralink thread placement. The interactive scene is not an anatomical placement map.
