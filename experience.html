Two attempts.
Two recorded results.

The homepage uses two selected trials from participant T12 in Stanford’s published speech BCI dataset. Both were recorded on June 14, 2022.

Optional English browser text-to-speech reads the final prediction. It is not a recording or reconstruction of T12’s voice.

What does the heatmap show?

Each row corresponds to one of 256 source channels, and time runs horizontally. We sample every 40 ms from source data binned every 20 ms, then use log scaling and per-trial contrast scaling for visibility. Brightness is not directly comparable between trials.

The source data contain many very low values in some channels. A dark region must not be interpreted as an inactive part of the brain. The heatmap is a view of recorded features, not an anatomical map.

Time alignment follows the source go-period boundaries. The complete trials contain 188 and 117 source time bins respectively.
