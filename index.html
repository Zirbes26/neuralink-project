NEURALINK’S SPEECH RESTORATION EXPLANATION

How it works

The speech motor cortex helps coordinate speaking. Neuralink’s implant records neural activity from this region.

A participant tries to speak or imagines speaking. A trained decoder interprets patterns in the recorded activity and produces a continuing sequence of words.

The output can appear on a device as text or be synthesized as speech. Neuralink describes a personalized voice option to support everyday conversation.
