This heatmap is supporting evidence. Moving its timeline inspects the signal only; it does not advance the guided journey. Horizontal axis: time. Vertical axis: 256 source channels. Brighter cells indicate higher scaled spike-band power.

The signal heatmap shows spike-band power from 256 channels. It is sampled every 40 ms here for a lighter web display; the source data were binned every 20 ms. Contrast is log-scaled for visibility. The words are the study’s recorded real-time n-gram decoder output. The target sentence and decoder result are shown separately because they can differ.

The study participant attempted speech. Neuralink was not involved in collecting this dataset. Audio is optional browser text-to-speech. It reads the recorded prediction in a standard English voice; it does not reconstruct T12’s voice. Available voices depend on your device and browser.
