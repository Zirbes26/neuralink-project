Before you explore a study

  • What is demonstrated, and what is still a research goal?
  • What training and ongoing support are involved?
  • Where are the official study eligibility and participation details?

What comes from where?

  • Inspiration and company information: Neuralink’s official website and video.
  • Neural features and recorded predictions: Stanford speech neuroprosthesis research, Willett et al. (2023), participant T12, Dryad sentences dataset.
  • Head, device and decoder visuals: educational illustrations, not the participant or a reconstruction of the study hardware.
  • Spoken output: optional English browser text-to-speech. No participant voice reconstruction is provided.

Hardware reference

The implant model explains the biocompatible enclosure, wirelessly rechargeable battery, processing electronics and flexible electrode threads described in Neuralink’s official technology overview. It is our own schematic geometry, not a product CAD model. It does not show verified thread locations, exact dimensions or an actual electrode count.

Research example: recording sites and references

The Stanford T12 placement and decoding roles follow Figure 1 and Extended Data Figures 2 and 7 of Willett et al. (2023), together with the sentences dataset’s channel map. The current 3D story is a conceptual illustration, not a reconstruction of T12’s recording sites. Neuralink speech restoration is a separate programme; its implant locations are not inferred from Stanford’s study.
