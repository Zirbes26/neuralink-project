Before you explore a study

  • What is demonstrated, and what is still a research goal?
  • What training and ongoing support are involved?
  • Where are the official study eligibility and participation details?

What comes from where?

  • Inspiration and company information: Neuralink’s official website and video.
  • Neural features and recorded predictions: Stanford speech neuroprosthesis research, Willett et al. (2023), participant T12, Dryad sentences dataset.
  • Head, device and decoder visuals: educational illustrations, not the participant or a reconstruction of the study hardware.
  • Spoken output: optional English browser text-to-speech. No participant voice reconstruction is provided.

About this project
