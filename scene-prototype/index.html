This is an independent educational prototype. Hardware and placement are illustrative. It is not a surgical guide.

Speech replay: Stanford / Willett et al. (2023), participant T12. These are recorded predictions, not a running decoder or Neuralink measurements. Computer audio is optional browser speech synthesis.

Face: Face Cap example model, credited by Three.js to Face Cap. Original eye and brow morphs are used. Jaw and lip morphs animate only during the scripted patient introduction; later computer output never animates the mouth. The source recording animation is never played. This avatar does not depict T12. Local evaluation prototype; asset redistribution rights require confirmation before public release.

Alex is a fictional character. Speech abilities vary among people with ALS. The introduction uses paced synthetic dialogue, not a clinical simulation of dysarthria. See The ALS Association and Neuralink’s investigational speech research. The scene does not promise a treatment or depict eligibility decisions.
