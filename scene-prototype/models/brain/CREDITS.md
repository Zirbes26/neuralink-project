# Brain surface

This work is based on "Brain" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/brain-cadd2bde67404c43b2359a6a3281d84a) by dgallichan (https://sketchfab.com/dgallichan), licensed under CC-BY-4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Original license.txt is retained as LICENSE.txt. The author describes the surface as generated with FreeSurfer from a T1-weighted MRI scan.

Changes: glTF/bin to GLB conversion, removal of unused vertex colour attributes, lossless 32-bit to 16-bit index compaction where supported, neutral rough material, scene orientation and illustrative fitting to a fictional avatar. All 377,701 triangles and original normals are preserved. This is not a clinical registration or a Neuralink implant placement map. The surface scan does not belong to the fictional character.
