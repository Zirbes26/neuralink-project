THE BASICS

A connection,
step by step.

Think of a brain-computer interface as a new route between certain brain activity and an external device. Here is the idea in three steps.

01 / RECORD

Neural activity

Implanted electrodes record activity in a specific area of the brain. This is not unrestricted access to everything a person thinks.

02 / DECODE

Learning a mapping

Software learns relationships between recorded activity and attempted actions or speech. Training and calibration matter.

03 / OUTPUT

Communication

A system can turn decoded output into text, computer actions, or synthesized speech, depending on its purpose.

Telepathy and Voice

Neuralink describes Telepathy as a computer-control application and Voice as its speech-restoration work. Camera-based lip reading is a different input method.

Research, not a promise

Speech restoration is being investigated in clinical research. A participant story does not establish the same outcome for every person. Consult the official study information for current details.
